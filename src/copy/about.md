I edit trailers, commercials, movies, TV series, documentaries — if it has moving
pictures, I cut it together. I’ve created work for clients like HBO Max, Netflix, Nike,
Adidas, Amazon Studios, Showtime, and A&E, among others.

The commercial side runs from nationally recognized brand campaigns to cutdowns and
social edits built to work vertically or at fifteen seconds. The narrative side runs
from trailers and full-length episodes through series and feature films. In the
unscripted world, I’ve created hour-long documentaries and cut hundreds of sizzles
responsible for dozens of sold projects.

I play well with others, but also work independently, start to finish: story, structure,
pacing, sound, color. Premiere and Resolve, mostly. Avid when asked nicely.

**Based in Portland. Working everywhere.**
