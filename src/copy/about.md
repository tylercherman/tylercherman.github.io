I cut trailers, commercials, TV series, films, and documentaries — if it has moving
pictures, I put it together. I've worked with clients like HBO Max, Netflix, Nike,
Adidas, Amazon Studios, Showtime, and A&E, among others. Recently that included the
trailer for an Oscar-nominated short film. Over more than a decade, by a conservative
count, several hundred sizzles.

The commercial side runs from sportswear and consumer campaigns to cutdowns and social
edits built to work vertically or at fifteen seconds. The narrative side runs from
trailers and pilots through series and hour-long documentary.

I work independently, start to finish: story, structure, pacing, sound, color. Premiere
and Resolve, mostly. Avid when asked nicely.

**Based in Portland. Working everywhere.**
