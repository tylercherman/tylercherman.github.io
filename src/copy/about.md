I edit trailers, commercials, movies, TV series, documentaries — If it moves, I cut it.
I’ve created work for clients like HBO Max, Netflix, Nike, Adidas, Amazon Studios,
Showtime, and A&E, among others.

The commercial side runs from nationally recognized brand campaigns to social edits
built to stop the scroll. The narrative side runs from trailers and full-length episodes
through series and feature films. In the unscripted world, I’ve created hour-long
documentaries and created hundreds of sizzles responsible for dozens of sold projects.

I play well with others, but also work independently, start to finish: story, structure,
pacing, sound, color. Premiere and Resolve, mostly. Avid when asked nicely.

**Based in Portland. Working everywhere.**
